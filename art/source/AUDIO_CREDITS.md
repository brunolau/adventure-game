## Music (original, AI-generated for this game)

Generated with Google **Lyria 3 Pro** via fal.ai (`fal-ai/lyria3/pro`, USD 0.08 per track, logged in
`art/spend-log.csv`), prompts in `art/tools/music.py`, raw files and the full request/response in
`art/masters/music/`. Mastered (loop points, one-beat baked crossfade, -16 LUFS, OGG Vorbis) by
`art/tools/audio_master.py`. Lyria output carries Google's inaudible SynthID watermark. The fal.ai /
Google terms grant the user the rights to generated output; no third-party melody was requested
(prompts describe moods and instruments only, never an existing piece or artist).
