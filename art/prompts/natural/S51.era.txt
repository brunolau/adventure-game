Era changes: the same stop on Thursday 29 October 2020, a sunny autumn afternoon: warm low sunlight, long soft shadows, the lindens and trees in yellow, orange and brown autumn colours, some fallen leaves on the platform and on the grass. The tram line was rebuilt in 2019-2020, so:
- The old brown tube shelter with its blue roof and wooden back wall is gone. In exactly its place and footprint stands a modern shelter: a slim dark grey steel frame, a flat clear glass roof, clear glass back and side walls with thin vertical white stripes, a modern grey metal bench inside along the back. Through its glass the road, the lawn and the trees stay visible. A small paper notice with a face-mask pictogram (no text) is stuck on the glass.
- The platform has new paving of light beige concrete tiles, a terracotta-red strip along its middle and a light tactile edge strip along the track; the low road-side wall is new light concrete with galvanised steel railings, but its old chipped end at the ramp is unchanged.
- The stop post is a modern dark grey post in the same place: on top the SAME round clock with its old polished brass frame (now around a new white dial), below the clock an LED departure board with orange dot-matrix dots that form no readable words or digits, and lower down a modern timetable panel with illegible print.
- The track is rebuilt: new rails on new concrete sleepers and fresh grey ballast, a low dark green mesh fence on short posts between the two tracks, new slim grey traction poles and overhead wires in the same places. NO tram: the near track in front of the platform is empty, so paint the track, the grass strip, the far road and the trees that were hidden behind the old tram.
- Two or three modern parked cars on the far road at the right (no number plates). No people.
Everything else built (the platform's position and edges, the ramp and zebra crossing, the road, the panel towers, the shopping pavilion) stays exactly where it is.
