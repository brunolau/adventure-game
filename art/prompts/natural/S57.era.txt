Era changes: the same place on an early-December afternoon in 1982, cold and calm: pale low winter sun through a thin haze, soft long shadows, bare grey-brown lindens, maples and birches, a thin layer of old snow lying on the lawns, along the kerbs, on the roofs and in the corners of the platform, the platform itself mostly clear and damp. The tram line to Dubravka does NOT exist yet - it is being built:
- The track bed at the right is a building site: fresh gravel and sand, a few of the big grey concrete track panels already laid on the near side, more concrete panels and rails stacked beside the far track, no rails on the far track yet, no overhead wires; the tall lamp posts stand, but no wires hang between them. A low red-and-white striped wooden construction barrier runs along the platform's right edge, with one small white board on it with the black words STAVBA ELEKTRIČKOVEJ TRATE.
- No tram: where the 1995 tram stood, paint the building site, the bare trees, the east carriageway and the medical centre behind it.
- The platform slabs are new concrete; the low concrete kerb block at the near left with its chipped corner, the ramp and the zebra crossing are the same.
- An older, simpler shelter stands in exactly the same place and footprint: a plain dark green steel frame with a flat roof and clear glass panels, a simple wooden bench inside; through its glass the road behind shows.
- On the west carriageway at the left, beside the platform, an articulated city bus of the period (Ikarus 280 type: boxy, cream-white upper body and dark red lower body, big flat windscreen, no logos, no number, blank destination box) waits at a temporary bus stop, its front towards the camera, its front part fully visible between the left frame edge and the shelter's near end, the rest hidden behind the shelter; a small round temporary bus-stop sign on a post beside it (bus pictogram only).
- The stop post in the same place carries the same round brass clock on top, a small white plate with a bus pictogram, and a small glazed case with a paper timetable.
- Beyond the building site, on the green strip in front of the medical centre, a large red slogan billboard on two legs faces the stop, with neat white capital letters: ZA SPOLOČNÚ BUDÚCNOSŤ.
- The low bluish two-storey medical centre on the right (its long white ribbon windows and blue-grey mosaic tiles) and the long panel block with the red-orange balconies behind it already stand, new and clean, in exactly the same places.
- One or two period cars (small boxy 1970s sedans, beige and pale blue) on the east carriageway at the right. No people.
