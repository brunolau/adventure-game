Era changes: the same school yard twenty-five years later, on a sunny late-October afternoon in 2020: warm low autumn sun, the trees of the estate behind the court, the poplar and the woods on the hill in yellow, orange and rust autumn colours, a few fallen yellow leaves on the asphalt. The court fence is newer: orange-painted steel frames with light wire mesh. The tall panel block at the right is insulated and repainted in pastel colours. No shade there.
