Era changes: the same school yard twenty-five years later, on a sunny late-October afternoon in 2020: warm low autumn sun, the trees beside the gym wing and the woods on the hill in yellow, orange and rust autumn colours, a few fallen yellow leaves on the asphalt and the lawn. The school is the same building with the same storeys, piers, door, canopy and steps, but renovated: the walls are now plain light grey with white bands, the windows are new white plastic windows, and the ground floor between the piers is painted with cheerful simple invented children's motifs (flowers, a sun, leaves) in bright colours; the gym wing is light grey with new white windows. The old red clay running track and its grass infield are still there, worn and faded, with weeds at the edges.
Behind the fence: the concrete basketball playground is GONE - the whole area behind the fence is now a small football pitch with bright green ARTIFICIAL TURF, crisp white painted pitch lines and a small white football goal with a net at its far end; NO basketball baskets, poles or backboards any more. The fence keeps every post and rail where it is, but it is renewed: dark green welded-wire mesh panels, and a tall dark green ball-stop net above the far end of the pitch.
On the yard the corner has been cleared: the art panel, the young linden, its grass bed with the kerb, the wooden bench and the low red-brick wall are ALL GONE - in their place is plain grey patched asphalt reaching back to the lawn, with a slightly darker, newer rectangle of asphalt where the wall stood; of the red water pipe only a short red-painted stub sticks out of the asphalt at the same spot. No shade there. The old round steel dustbin by the fence is replaced by a green plastic wheelie bin with a lid at the same spot (prop 2).
