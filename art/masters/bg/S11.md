## Geometry (the L_STOP camera)

Camera ~3.5 m above the SW (school-side) platform, looking straight across the double track (side-on, tracks
horizontal), horizon above the frame. Bands, top to bottom: ground floor of the 1980s shopping pavilion (y 0-95),
grass strip with young lindens, far pavement and carriageway (y 150-250), low green railing (y ~250), far platform
with the long shelter and the stop post (y 256-412), far platform edge (y 412-426), far track (y 426-518), median with
a traction pole and a green railing (y 518-602), near track (y 602-750, near rail at y 680), our platform edge stones
(y 750-766), our platform = walk band (y 766-1080), with a lowered granite kerb running diagonally from (182,750) to
(102,1080) and an asphalt footway beyond it at the far left. The template prop row y 150-250 is the far platform,
which is why the timetable and the bench are across the track and the clock (anchor y 320) stands on our side.

Anchors (`game.json landmark_layouts.L_STOP`, magenta crosses in the overlay), all hit:
- `curb` (150, 890): the chipped outer corner of the lowered kerb (broken notch x ~120-160, y ~845-900, one small
  fragment on the paving at ~(195, 905)) - made by the v2 fix.
- `track_line` (960, 680): the near rail of the near track runs horizontally through y 680 across the whole width.
- `clock_frame` (1560, 320): centre of the round brass clock frame (outer diameter ~110 px, white dial, black
  ticks, no numerals) on a short dark green cast-iron column standing at our platform edge (foot at y ~770).

- Style: painterly, consistent with the art/backgrounds references.

## How to derive S51 (2020) and S57 (1982) from this base

Edit this master (`refit_room.py fix` with boxes, or a full repaint that sends `S11_v3.png` as image 1 instead of the
sketch); never move the fixed parts.

Keep pixel-identical across eras: track bed, both rails (near rail y 680), median traction pole at x ~1015, platform
edge stones, our platform's outline and the lowered kerb with its chip at (150, 890), the clock's brass frame centred
at (1560, 320) (S51: "starý rám ostáva súčasťou hodín" - the 2020 clock body may be new, the brass frame stays), the
far platform outline and railing line, the carriageway, the pavilion's ground-floor line.

Change per era (`location_families.L_STOP`):
- **S51, 29 Oct 2020** (reconstruction finished 26 Oct 2020): new paving on our platform with tactile strips at the
  edge (keep the kerb chip - "Ten roh je stále odštiepený"), a new glass shelter with an LED departure board, a modern
  low-floor tram or none, autumn yellow lindens and leaves, masks (a dropped mask, a mask notice), modern cars on
  the carriageway, a reworked clock in the old brass frame.
- **S57, early Dec 1982**: bare branches and a thin patchy snow, an older shelter, a paper timetable, a period
  slogan board, coats; the tram itself is on hold (ISSUES.md ART-DUBEXT-01 - the line did not reach Dúbravka before
  1984). The 1980s concrete slabs and the brass clock frame can stay.
- Rect conflict for both: see ISSUES.md ART-STOP-01 (S51/S57 hotspot rects vs the family anchors and this layout).
