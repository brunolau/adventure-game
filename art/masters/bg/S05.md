  (x 1018-1138 vs rect 1035-1155); vine: leaves and grapes above the fence, rect nudged -40 px onto them.

- Real place: Pezinská house types - single-storey house with pale peach and light blue plaster and lace curtains, green
  picket fence on a low stone wall, brick-paved pavement with gravel and grass verge, grey concrete electricity pole.
  The raised plot with steps is the pilot's invention to satisfy the template rects (ISSUES.md ART-BLOCK-01).
- Composition caveat: because the props must be in the top quarter, the frontage is cropped at the top (gate canopy and
  roofs are out of frame) and the lower half is pavement.

## Property continuity for S06, S09, S10 (same house)

Mira's plot is ~1 m above the street behind a rough stone retaining wall with five worn stone steps on the left up to a
narrow green plank gate between whitewashed pillars (tiled gate canopy, two-tone bell on the right pillar). Right of the
gate a small red-brick garden workshop with a weathered vertical-plank door, black strap hinges and a padlock. A green
wooden picket fence runs along the wall; an old grapevine on a wooden trellis grows behind it; the house is single-storey
with pale peach (left) and light blue (right) plaster, white-framed windows with lace curtains and brown roller-blind
boxes. Autumn 2020: yellowing vine leaves, dark grapes.

Ambient candidates: vine leaves and grapes (wind), falling leaves on the pavement, leaf shadows, a curtain moving behind
the window, birds on the electricity wires (out of frame above the pole).
