## Camera (measured on the painting)

Two-point perspective of the walled court: the right wall's top and foot meet at VP (-1146, ~478), the back wall's at
(2836, ~470); horizon y ~475, focal ~1988 px for a principal point at x 960 (the two orthogonal vanishing points), a
nearly level camera standing on the lawn outside the near wall. The camera height came from the objects the edit
painted (the bench seat 0.45 m) and the walls: **~2.45 m**, the right wall ~1.3 m, the low back wall ~0.7 m, the near
wall ~1.4 m, the passage ~1.1 m wide, the court ~9 m along the back wall. px per metre at a ground point =
(y - 475) / 2.45; **hero scale (y - 475) / 729** -> walk_band [701, 1015], actor_scale [0.31, 0.74]. The template's
estimate (0.21-0.55) was too small: the figures read right against the bench, the bike, the panels and the passage
at 0.31-0.74 (checked in-engine). No repaint of a closer framing was needed (SOKOLIKOVA_YARD.md 1.1 open question).

### The added things (all minimal, no lettering)

- **Green bench** (look.S69.bench "čerstvo natretá"): painted by the edit, kept in place.
- **Kubo's bike**: small red frame, black saddle, stabilisers, silver handlebar; the bell is too small at game size to
  show a missing cap (the look says it).
- **The school club's painting** on the three right-wall panels next to the passage (x 1381-1549, y 586-706), drawn
  locally in emulsion-on-concrete style (the panel's light and texture show through): panel 1 the four shapes (red
  circle, blue triangle, yellow square, purple V = the S17 sign's shapes), panel 2 **three waves** (a green zigzag with
  three peaks) over **two black strokes**, panel 3 **six red pieces** (squares, two bent pieces, one curved piece):
  exactly the P03 clue 3-2-6 of `look.S69.rhythm`, no digits and no words.
- **Kubo's chalk tally** (days to the holidays) low on a panel further right (x 1612-1672).
- **Chalk hopscotch** on the asphalt (8 squares: 1, 2, 3, 4-5, 6, 7-8 and the half circle; 0.8 x 3.0 m on the ground
  plane, warped with the measured camera, quad x 1098-1462 y 814-894), grainy chalk, no numbers.
