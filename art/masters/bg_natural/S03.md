## Real place

The playground **Lúčny koník** in the Rekreapark by Javorová alej, Čierna Voda (opened 2015): the open timber picnic
shelter with a four-sided hip roof of wooden shingles on round log posts with curved knee braces, a natural-stone block
with the grill and the chimney at one end, heavy timber tables, multi-coloured concrete paving only under and around
the shelter, the wooden log playground (climbing tower with a small shingle roof, slide, balance beams), a standing
stone, young trees on the meadow, flat fields and low hills behind.

- Generator input (CC BY-SA 4.0): `art/source/rooms/S03/rekreapark_shelter_panoramax.jpg` (p4n-pics, Panoramax,
  21 May 2022) - the same shelter, seen from the meadow.
- Owner reference, LOOKED AT ONLY (copyrighted blog photo, June 2018, never sent to a generator, not redistributed):
  `art/source/owner_refs/web_lucny_konik_2018.jpg`. It confirms the roof shape, the honey-coloured log posts, the
  stone block with the chimney at the right end, the paving pattern, the standing stone and the log playground, and
  that the shelter existed before October 2020.
- Not painted: the playground's giant wooden grasshopper climbing structure (designed by a sculptor = third-party
  artwork, ART_DIRECTION section 2; since v6 a generic, invented wooden grasshopper stands in its place, see above), the red no-dogs sign on the stone, the car, the
  table-tennis table and the street lamp of the photo. No lettering.
- Style reference: the natural S05 (same prologue, same light).
