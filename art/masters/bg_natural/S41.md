## Real place and sources

The real view **in front of Hotel Pošta at Biela Púť, Jasná**: the access road leading in from the foreground, the
arched wooden footbridge with its X-lattice railing over the stream on the left, the stone-clad retaining wall along
the right side of the road, the gondola valley station (white-grey rope turnaround housing, low boarding hall) behind
a wooden fence, the steel tube pylon at the right edge with the two ropes, the steep-gabled timber chalet of Hotel
Pošta in the middle distance, spruce forest and hills, the slope on the right.

Image inputs (owner feedback 2026-10-05, newest section): the owner's reference photos `imgur_TXI6i3E.jpg` (the exact
view, summer) and `imgur_8l08ndF.jpg` (the same hotel and station a few dozen metres further on) come from **a friend
of the product owner's private archive, used with permission** - they may be passed to the generator. They stay local:
`art/source/owner_refs/` is git-ignored, nothing of them was copied into `art/source/rooms/` or any committed folder.
The aligned crop used as image 2 (`art/source/owner_refs/_S41_aligned_TXI6i3E.png`, game = (photo - (244, 124)) * 1.15)
is in the same ignored folder. Credit: "reference photos: a friend of the product owner, used with permission" (no
name). Style reference: `art/backgrounds/tram-stop.png` (no winter Jasná room existed yet when this was painted).

## Camera and blocking

Camera = the photographer's: about 2.8 m above the road (measured from the people in the photo: two figures give
horizon y ~347 and height/(feet - horizon) = 0.61), looking along the road. Perspective s(y) = (y - 347) / 839:
`walk_band` [612, 1015], `actor_scale` [0.32, 0.80] (hero 410 px at the bottom, 164 px at the far walkable end).
Checked on the painting: road 5.0 m wide at y 1000 and 4.9 m at y 700, the card reader 1.3 m, the gate posts 1.1 m,
the exhibition pillar 1.9 m.

- Walk polygon: the ploughed asphalt only, fitted to the painted snow banks (left edge from (565, 624) to (93, 1015),
  right edge along the wall-foot snow ridge, a small bay at the gate mouth). Spawn (820, 900).
- `S41.ambient 1` Lavičky: two snow-capped benches on the cleared left verge between the road and the stream.
- `S41.ambient 2` Tabuľa smerov: three arrow boards on a post standing on the wall top at the far edge of the steps'
  gap; label below the boards (above them it would collide with the station exit label).
- `S41.gate` Čítačka platného lístka: a fictional pedestrian access to the station: a gap in the retaining wall with
  three granite steps up to the snow field, the dark card-reader pillar (green light) on the road side and a stainless
  turnstile with glass wings across the gap. The hero stands left of it on the road (1060, 750) and reaches right, so
  the reader stays visible (staging rule M5-04 / PT-S06). This gap is the only invented change to the real view.
- `S41.ROBOT`: actor variant **`snow`** (new, `src/game/assets/actors/ROBOT/actor.json`): the same sheets with a little
  fresh snow on the lid (`npc_sheet_snow.webp`, `idle_sheet_snow.webp`, made by `S41_winter/robot_snow.py`; the open-lid
  gesture frame keeps a thin strip on the lid edge); feet (600, 760) at the left edge of the road, scale 0.50 by
  perspective, z auto. ROBOT appears only in S41.
- `time_node` (S41 is an anchor node, game.json `anchor_nodes`): own rect [438, 430, 84, 216] on the **fictional Atlas
  exhibition stand** (light-wood pillar with a round turquoise-rimmed clock face, on the verge at the bridge head),
  interaction point (560, 662). Like S11/S57: hover "Hodiny časového uzla – výber obdobia", a click walks there and
  opens the era chooser; only while Core lists portal targets (verified after C05, `build/screens/s41_winter/after_C05_*`).
  ISSUES PT-F10 said "S41 has no painted clock (HUD/T only)" - that is no longer true.
- Exits: `S41.to_S42` = the far end of the road in front of the hotel (towards Hotel Grand Jasná), interaction point
  at the far end of the walkable road (800, 630); `S41.to_S67` = the station building, interaction point at the gate
  mouth (1120, 745). Both warnings of `check_blocking.py` ("interaction point 128 / 386 px from its rect") are
  intended: the targets are far away in this real view and are reached by walking to the end of the road / through
  the gate.
- Audio override in the blocking (`ambience_mode: replace`): cable_car_hum -4 dB, tourists -10 dB, **wind_mountain**
  -12 dB instead of the summer `leaves_wind` of game.json `rooms[].ambience`.

## Ambient (17 layers, `data/blocking/ambient/S41.json`)

Light snowfall (55 far flakes behind the actors + 12 big near flakes in front); four gondola cabins (two per rope,
staggered, 26-30 px/s) riding the ropes between the top edge and the station, scaled for depth 1.25 -> 0.70 and fading
in/out at the station (sprite `S41/natural/cabin_winter.webp` = the parked cabin of the painting cut at master
resolution, with a hanger extension and grip, pivot on the grip); tiny skiers down the groomed piste behind the
station and down the snowy slope on the right (`skier_a/b/c.webp`, drawn locally); fine snow powder sifting off the
eaves of the low building and the hotel wing; steam from the three chimneys of the hotel's left wing; wind sway of the
snow-laden spruces (by the footbridge, by the hotel, on the slope); ripples and glints on the dark stream; the green
light of the card reader pulsing. Cut-outs from v6 by `ambient_cut.py --natural S41`; `make_sprites.py` makes the
cabin and skiers.
`S41/natural/cloud_a.webp` (the summer cumulus other 2035 rooms used as a shared sprite) was NOT re-cut and its
manifest entry is kept; the obsolete summer cut-outs (sky_mask, spruces_right, grass_verge; only S41 used them) were
removed.
