## Method

`derive_era.py run S17 S61 --from 2` (era prompt `art/prompts/natural/S61.era.txt`): same camera, December 1982 (bare
trees, thin snow, pale low sun), school 8 years old with plain fresh pale-yellow render, no art panel yet, the hand cart
in its place, the young sapling, the brand-new brick wall with the open niche (stone of row 2 / column 3 on the coping),
the freshly painted pipe. Camera measure: global shift 0; building_corner, service_niche, pipe_axis, both side exits 0 px.
tree_trunk "MOVED +16" and to_S64 "MOVED" are content changes (new tree, cart tracks), not drift.

## Review

Props in their rects; asphalt band open (wet, snow patches, court lines); no people, lettering or cars. In-engine:
`build/screens/m3/S61/entry_*` and `after_E08_E09_*` (both overlays visible, HARNESS "variant visible"); patch borders
differ from the base by ~3 grey levels (invisible). Ambient (5 layers): snow (larger, opaque flakes so they read on the
pale sky), hazy clouds (sky mask), chimney smoke from three houses on the hill, crows, sparrows on the asphalt by the
wall (critters, flee). Snow/smoke parameters were tuned after the motion shots (first values were invisible).
