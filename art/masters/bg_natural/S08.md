## Real place

Owner feedback 2026-10-05: a natural pond with reeds and pale water in hummocky dry-grass scrubland, **no fence around
the pond**, low hills. **Owner correction 2026-10-06:** "there should be pumptrack on the other side of the lake
instead of houses ... it's a hill, not a totally flat one". So the pump track 'Kopec zábavy' is the raised hill across
the pond, and the camera stands on the near (west) bank, where the cycle path ends (the end-of-cycle-path sign), looking
ENE over the pond to the hill - the direction of the original OSM camera proposal in
`art/source/rooms/S08/layout_javorova-alej-pond_osm.png`.

References: the register's two Panoramax frames of the park edge (CC BY-SA 4.0, p4n-pics), the OSM layout, style =
natural S03 (same park, same light). Owner photos: `art/source/owner_refs/imgur_2tyAMxf.jpg` (the pond and the pump
track from the track, spring; owner permission 2026-10-05, used as image 3 of the v3 edit, only for the look of the
narrow dirt track over grassy hummocks) and `art/source/owner_refs/web_pumptrack_cierna_voda_2020.jpg` (a blog photo
from inside the pump track, Sept 2020: copyrighted, **looked at only**, never a generator input). What the blog photo
shows and v3 follows: a raised, hummocky dirt mound with a winding packed-dirt track over rollers and small berms
between tufts of grass and weeds; further away a long low white two-storey building and village houses, hills on the
horizon.

`note` texts of the blocking were updated to the new reading of the place).

## Ambient (12 layers)

clouds (sky mask, alpha 0.4; the mask now follows the skyline of the hill, the white building and the trees and never
reaches below y 172, so clouds stay off the distant hills); water ripples and glints; two ducks swimming on the pond
(water mask); sway on the far reeds (cut re-done from v3 and starting at y 270, below the track, so the track never
sways), the near reeds, the ferns (the ball's spot excluded) and the dry grass left of the bench; **three riders on the
pump track** (below); leaves blowing across the trail; birds over the fields. All cut-outs re-cut from v3
(`ambient_cut.py --natural S08`).

Riders (`tween_path`, plane back, drawn after the far reeds, hidden in reduced motion): a girl (~4, dark-blue jacket,
light-blue helmet, red wooden balance bike), a boy (~5, orange-red jacket, white helmet, blue balance bike) and a boy
(~10, yellow jacket, black helmet, black BMX). Sheets `assets/ambient/S08/natural/rider_{girl,boy,bmx}_sheet` (2/2/3
frames: push/glide, pedal_0/pedal_1/pump) are cut at on-screen size (balance-bike children ~32 px, BMX boy ~46 px,
i.e. ~27 px per metre on the hill; drawn at scale 1.0), toned 14 % towards the hill colour for distance, with a soft
contact shadow to the right (sun on the left); pivot = wheel contact. All three ride one way round the track (as on a
real pump track) along its traced centre line (`S08_pumptrack/track.py`, 88 points, 1822 px; traced on 4x grids and
corrected with brightness peaks of the painted ribbon): from the end of the bowl's inner strand, round the banked turn,
over the rollers and the crest, the hairpin, down the switchback and round the right berm, fading in/out (22 px) where
the painted strands end in the grass. Speeds: balance bikes 42/46 px/s (~40 s per lap), BMX 105 px/s (~17 s), waits
6-14 / 8-18 / 18-40 s; the BMX may overtake the small ones (it rides 1-2 px lower = the near side of the track, drawn
on top). Nobody comes near the walk band.
