## Real place and sources

The **old Starý most** in its 1995 state: the riveted steel truss bridge rebuilt after 1945 (Most Červenej armády),
demolished 2013 and replaced by today's bridge (2016). Facts checked on sk.wikipedia: railway part in service until 1983,
trams only 1951-1961, cars until 31 Dec 2008, buses until May 2010. In 1995 it carried cars and buses, the railway part was
disused, and there were **no trams** (the brief's "tram crossing" is therefore not used; cars cross Most SNP instead).
OSM: the bridge runs at a bearing of ~3° from (48.1359, 17.1170) to (48.1401, 17.1173); the upstream (west) side carries
the footway outside the road truss (Aktron's 2008 photo looks south along it, the river on the right).

Camera position: the upstream footway above the first river pier near the Petržalka bank (~48.1378, 17.1170), looking
**upstream** WNW. The castle (bearing ~292°), St Martin's (~298°) and Most SNP (pylon ~269°) all lie upstream of the
Starý most, so the view cannot look downstream as the dispatch text suggested; looking upstream gives Most SNP left of
centre, the castle in the centre, the cathedral and the Old Town riverfront on the right, which matches the 2011 photo
taken from this bridge. The distant panorama is enlarged against the foreground (as a long lens would show it).

## Review of the final

- Real place recognisable: the old bridge's riveted grey ironwork, plank footway and railing; the 1995 skyline (castle
  colours of the 1990s, no glass towers, no 2000s riverfront buildings, no hotel ships, no cranes, no billboards).
- Text: only the three zeros (and 3-2-6 in the B22 state); no lettering on the booth or the boat, no flags with emblems.
- No people or animals in the painting (the walkers and gulls are ambient layers).
- Liberties: the panorama is enlarged against the foreground; the right truss panel (X brace) stands at the back edge of
  the footway while the real footway was cantilevered outside the truss - read as framing, not as the footway's position.
