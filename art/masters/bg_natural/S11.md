## Why the redesign (owner Dubravka corrections, 2026-10-06)

`art/feedback/2026-10-05_owner_feedback.md`, newest section: S11/S51/S57 are the real **Švantnerova stop on
M. Schneidra-Trnavského**. v1 looked along a flat median platform with a generic shopping pavilion. The owner's corrections:
the street **rises slightly uphill** into the distance; the **tram tracks run to the right of the road** (1995: the old
concrete-panel track bed, a red railing); the panel blocks laid out as in the references (tall blocks with red/orange
balconies behind and on the right); on the **right a low bluish medical centre** (there since the early 1980s). The 1995
shelter stays the long curved brown/burgundy tube shelter with the translucent blue roof (owner note 2026-10-05).

## Right-side tweak (owner, 2026-10-06: "the medical centre should be moved lower - in reality only part of it should be visible ... the houses on the right side have to be taller, they are 8-storey panelaks")

Tool `art/prompts/natural/L_STOP.right_tweak.py` (shared by S11/S51/S57; docstring = the recipe). Compared side by side with
`imgur_gnv1VFF.jpg` (generator input, image 3 of the paid edits) and the imhd.sk photos (looked at only):
`art/review/natural/svantnerova_tweak/compare_refs.png` (git-ignored, contains the look-only photos).

- **Edit region**: game x >= 1262 above the crest of the verge beyond the east carriageway (crest y 432 at x 1262 ->
  478 at x 1920) + 14 px, 12 px soft seam. Everything else is v3 pixel for pixel (checked: 0 changed px in the timetable,
  bench, both platform exits, the clock / time node, the stop-post occluder and the whole walk band; the master changed only
  inside game x 1257-1927, y 0-497).
- **Medical centre lower**: every column moved down by 0.5 x its painted height (exact projection of a building whose
  ground is ~half its height below the road), clipped at the verge crest: only the flat roof edge, the upper ribbon windows
  and a strip of the mosaic tiles show; the lower floor is behind the verge (as in the friend's photo). Low shrubs and the
  grassy crest in front of it.
- **8-storey panelak**: a further-away, longer slab in the L_STOP camera (lines converge at the painted VP 812,408; roof
  line through (1288,190), leaving the frame at x ~1726; far end at x 1288): ground floor (behind the medical centre's
  roof) + 8 floors of recessed loggias with red-orange parapets + attic, counted on the result (8 parapet rows at x 1330,
  1800). The model repainted the slab with too big floors twice, so its own painted facade (attempt 110601) is
  re-projected at 0.622x (in w = 1/(x-VPX), m = (y-VPY)/(x-VPX) a receding facade is axis-aligned and periodic: one clean
  loggia + window module, two clean floors, the attic band) - exact perspective, painted texture.
- Lamp posts, lamp heads and the overhead wires in front of the slab kept as painted; the trees at the far end of the
  medical centre continued from the painting's own trees (mirrored at x 1262).
- **The T3** (painted at the platform) is pixel-exact (alpha of `tram_t3_body.webp`; 241,724 master px with alpha 1 have
  max diff 0); the edit painted what is behind it. Its exit rect S11.to_S19 contains 3,028 changed px - the background
  around the tram front (x 1256-1357, y 300-458), never the tram. The departing / arriving T3 sprites are unchanged
  (they never were in the edited area except passing in front of it); **`track_empty.webp` was rewritten** (same alpha,
  new background from the tramless view `_S11_v4_empty.png`), so no ghost of the old building shows when the tram is away

- Cuts: `sky_mask` now excludes the new slab by a polygon along its roof line; all sprites re-cut (`ambient_cut.py
  --natural S11`, manifest fresh).
