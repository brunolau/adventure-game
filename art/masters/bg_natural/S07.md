## Real place (owner feedback 2026-10-05)

The owner asked for the real **roadside stop on the long straight road at the edge of Čierna Voda** (two-lane road
into the distance, a lay-by with a small red-framed glass shelter, a low modern flat-roofed white/grey building behind
young trees, wide grass verges). The owner's Street View screenshot (`art/source/owner_refs/imgur_wrRxN8x.jpg`) was used
only as a location pointer. Searching KartaView sequence 11670418 (synalik, CC BY-SA 4.0) around every OSM bus stop
of Čierna Voda found exactly this type at the **"Chorvátsky Grob, Triangel"** stop (OSM nodes 2609802969 / 2609802978,
48.2325 N 17.2572 E): red-framed glass shelters with curved white roofs on lay-bys on both sides, wide verges, young
trees, flat-roofed white business buildings and the Little Carpathians on the horizon. New source photos (downscaled to
1920 px): `art/source/rooms/S07/triangel_stop_kartaview_281.jpg` (photo id 2630554650, shelter on the right, lay-by,
platform) and `triangel_stop_kartaview_277.jpg` (photo id 2630554646, the road, the opposite shelter, the hills).
Commons geosearch (2 km), Panoramax and Openverse had nothing closer.
