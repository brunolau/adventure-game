This is a TONE PASS over the whole scene, including lines that were approved before. Besides the usual checks, judge whether every reviewed line fits the owner's binding tone (Polda-style humour):
- lively character comedy: playful, a little absurd, situational jokes that make sense right here, the quirks, hobby-horses and running gags of the speaker (see VOICES); both sides of a conversation may be funny, the NPCs are not straight men for Adam;
- every line answers or reacts to what was just said and is something this person would really say now, in natural spoken Slovak.

Flag (verdict "fix") with category "flat" a line in a conversation, look or first-entry line that is dull or generic where this character in this situation could naturally be lively (a missed reaction, a bland filler line, a look that only states the obvious).
Flag with category "joke" a line that is the old pattern the owner called cringy: an ironic one-liner summary or wink that only comments on the previous line or closes the exchange (e.g. "Tak aspoň jedna krivka ide dobrým smerom.", "Aspoň niečo dnes funguje na prvý pokus."), a forced pun, a smug or try-hard quip, a joke that needs an explanation or does not make sense.
For each such flag, the suggestion is a complete Polda-style alternative: natural spoken Slovak, same speaker, every fact, name, number and clue of the original kept, same ty/vy, at most about 110 characters, one or two short sentences.

Do NOT flag: plain lines that carry a clue, a hint, an instruction or a serious beat (covid itself, the elderly Mira's tiredness, Tóno recognising Adam, the farewell lines) - those stay simple; a plain line that does its job inside an exchange that is already lively; a line only because you would phrase it differently. Not every line needs a joke - a conversation needs rhythm, so flag at most the lines that really drag the scene down.
