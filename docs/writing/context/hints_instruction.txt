THESE TEXTS ARE HINTS, NOT DIALOGUE. They are notes in Adam's notebook (Adam Hruška, 35, audio repairman from 2020,
first person, modern colloquial Slovak, short sentences, dry self-irony; he addresses nobody, so there is no ty/vy).
The player opens the hint screen when stuck. Per step there are two texts:
- hint.nudge.<step> (Standard and Hard difficulty, level 1): points the player's thinking (what is missing, who might
  know, what looks odd) WITHOUT naming the exact item combination or action. Lightly humorous is fine, a meta joke
  (about games, puzzles, inventory, players, hints) is not. It must still be useful on its own.
- hint.where.<step> (Standard, level 2): where to look (room or person), WITHOUT the exact action or item. On a puzzle
  step it explains the rule of the puzzle without the answer. On a bag step it only says it happens in the bag (brašňa).
The "Exact step" lines are context only. A suggestion must not reveal more of the exact step than the text does.
Knowledge rule (binding): the writer checked that each hint names only people, places and items the player already
knows at that step. A suggestion must NOT add any person, place, item, code or number that the reviewed text does not
already contain. Length: at most 140 characters, aim for 110. Fragments such as "V garáži." are fine for the
"where" texts. Flag only real problems (grammar, unnatural Slovak, nonsense, a hint that reveals the exact step, a
hint that is too vague to help at all); do not flag a text only because you would phrase it differently.
