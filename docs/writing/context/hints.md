# hints – Standard / Hard step hints (draft, owner approval pending)

Adam's notebook voice (first person, his 2020 Slovak, he addresses nobody). Two texts per step of the hint screen:
`hint.nudge.<step>` (Standard and Hard level 1): points the player's thinking (what is missing, who might know,
what looks odd) WITHOUT the exact item combination or action; may be lightly humorous, never a meta joke.
`hint.where.<step>` (Standard level 2): where to look (room or person), without the exact action or item; on a
puzzle step it explains the rule of the puzzle without the answer; on a bag step it says it happens in the bag.
Each at most 140 characters (aim 110). The exact Easy step is given as context only: it must NOT be revealed.
Knowledge rule: a hint names only people, places and items the player already knows at that step; the writer
checked this, so a suggestion must not add a name, place or item that the text does not already contain.
