## What I changed and why

**Grammar, calques, idiom**
- `action.I01.004`: "bude menej o koži a nitoch" is an English calque. It now reads "pri cestovaní v čase bude menej kože a nitov".
- `action.I16.001`: "Pridelím … dnešný dátum a pečiatku" is not idiomatic. Alojz now gives a terse clerk's list ("Evidenčné číslo Z-17, dnešný dátum, pečiatka.").
- `action.I15.001`: the verbless fragment is now a sentence with a verb ("Vložím uhlový papier medzi hárky…").
- `look.S38.ambient 1`: added the missing reflexive ("sa musí niekde začať").
- `look.S61.ambient 1`: fixed a comma splice. The hint that the wall rows are the reliable landmark stays.
- `look.S60.DOBRO`: "Pozorne, ale nie zle" is not idiomatic. Now "Prísne, ale so záujmom."
- `item.CUFF_OPEN`: an infinitive fragment, now in Adam's first-person voice.
- `topic.SIMON.ambient 2.label`: word order ("Prečo ide vozík tadiaľto?").

**Lines that did not follow from the line before**
- `action.Q6B.002`: "návod, ktorému rozumie aj holub" made no sense, because the instruction is for Adam. Now: "Dva kroky a žiaden lístok. To zvládnem." This plays on Štefan's slips.
- `topic.POSTA.ambient 2.002`: "Taká zásielka…" had nothing to refer to. Adam now reacts to Alojz's "radosť s krídlami".
- `topic.RUZENA.ambient 2`: Ružena speaks first, and her line does not answer the label "Máte zaváracie poháre?". The label is now "Koľko vydrží taký pohár?" (the jar is visible). The reply "Tak ho treba dobre schovať." works before and after E05; the old reply assumed Adam already had the jar.
